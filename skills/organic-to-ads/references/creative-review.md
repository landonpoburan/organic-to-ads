# Complete the creative before the final paid recommendation

The metric scan selects candidates. The creative review determines what the advertiser should reuse, adapt, or develop. Keep these stages distinct while completing both when the request is a full review.

## Establish the real source

Match the actual post to its saved permalink/identifier. Read the caption, but identify the topic and hook from the asset. Repeated introductions, boilerplate offer paragraphs, and recurring hashtags are context, not proof of the video's subject or performance mechanism. Comments can reveal questions or resource interest; they do not substitute for the spoken argument.

For each shortlisted asset, record a compact evidence map:

| Part | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Opening | First frame, first spoken/on-screen line, or first slide; distinguish what is promised from the topic alone. |
| Argument/demo | The complete spoken or written sequence; what changes the viewer's understanding? |
| Proof | Demonstration, cited evidence, supported experience, or testimonial actually supplied. Describe what is presented as proof without independently validating sources or certifying claims. |
| Close | Final spoken/on-screen or closing-slide next step, plus the caption CTA. They may differ or only the caption may contain one. |
| Audience response | Available comment examples, saves/shares and rates, and any actual enquiries/results. Keep keyword responses distinct from qualified leads. |

## Video and carousel coverage

For video, review the full spoken content through playback or an existing/matched transcript. Inspect the opening visuals, proof/demo moments, and close. A transcript establishes spoken content; it does not establish editing, pacing, visual proof, or on-screen text. Verify important lines and unclear automated transcription against playback or visible subtitles when possible. Do not build an ad around uncertain wording.

For a carousel, inspect every accessible slide in order, then the caption and CTA. A retrieved child-media link is not an inspected slide. For an image, inspect the actual visual and all relevant text, not only alt text or the caption.

Use tools already available in the host. Before optional download, transcription, or frame extraction, check required binaries, libraries, and speech models. Report a missing dependency before starting that route, and use an available alternative when practical. Do not silently install tools, download a model, or send user media to a new transcription service. Keep authorized review assets/transcripts outside the repository and installed skill.

When a route fails, try another authorized route that can resolve the specific gap, such as the returned media link, accessible browser playback, or an existing transcript. Do not repeat the same failure or turn it into an empty successful review. If no route works, give the provisional shortlist and exact next evidence needed. Never imply the full review was completed.

## Explain what might have worked

Use source locations: a timestamp, slide number, or actual caption/comment passage. Separate observations from plausible causes. Compare at least one weaker relevant asset where available. If both posts use the same boilerplate caption, examine the difference in creative rather than claiming that shared paragraph caused the winner.

Check whether the strongest measured signal matches the explanation. A comment-driven question is different from a saved demonstration. Stronger reach-based rates can strengthen a resonance hypothesis, while distribution, age, timing, and sample limits remain relevant. A large comment count alone cannot establish commercial intent.

## Produce a decision for each ad use

Assess the actual asset for relevant awareness/video views, warm-audience building, nurture of an existing warm audience, Instagram-profile follower growth, engagement/message-led attention or leads, and direct response where appropriate. Recommend only uses with a plausible connection to the business and source content. A strong educational reel can be tested unchanged for viewing/engagement and adapted with a CTA for enquiries.

An Instagram-profile growth test can reuse relevant existing content without a training CTA; a follow prompt is optional. A message-led lead test should identify the CTA, actual resource/offer, and manual or existing automated response journey. Do not add a DM tactic to every post simply because it is an available use.

For warm-audience nurture, assess whether the existing content helps an already-familiar audience understand the business, perspective, or useful expertise. An offer CTA is optional, and reuse may be enough. Do not collapse this into audience building or immediate-conversion retargeting. Identify the available audience from supplied context and judge relevant engagement/viewing without claiming those metrics directly prove trust.

For each recommended use, state:

- **Preserve:** the actual opening, argument, demonstration, or delivery worth carrying into paid use, with its source location.
- **Change, if needed:** the exact structure, length, readability, or CTA adjustment for that use. Locate the original CTA in the asset, caption, or both. No offer CTA is required for a video-view use; follower growth is not guaranteed by viewing.
- **Route and readiness:** test existing asset, edit, or re-record; give readiness for this use alone. Prefer the smallest change. A new closing shot or CTA overlay may be sufficient for direct response. Re-record only when the requested execution requires material changes.
- **Next step:** the concrete action and the test outcome it enables. A reuse path and an adaptation path can coexist.

Do not validate claims/sources, audit policy compliance, or introduce rights/music checks as review criteria. Source fidelity still matters: accurately report what the asset contains, and do not fabricate or amplify statements in expansion drafts. Remove irrelevant content only when it improves the proposed creative use, not as an unsolicited factual audit.

Keep supported pursuit priority when readiness for a particular use is incomplete. Missing access may make the direction provisional; identify exactly what remains unseen. Finish with an honest coverage statement: which candidates received full spoken/written review and visual checks, which are partial, and whether baseline or business context is still missing. Complete accessible review before delivery.
